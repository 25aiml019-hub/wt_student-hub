About StudentHub

A simple, student-built portal for campus information.

Our goal

StudentHub is an online platform that helps students stay on top of campus life. It brings events, registrations, profiles and feedback together so nothing important gets missed.

We want learning and campus activity to feel simple, organised and accessible for every student, at any time of day.

Built as a Web Development Fundamentals project using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, PHP and MySQL.

What you can do

📅 Discover events

Search, filter and register for upcoming campus events.

👤 Manage your profile

Keep contact details, department and hobbies current.

💬 Share feedback

Tell the team what works and what should improve.
