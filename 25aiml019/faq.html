Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about accounts, events and your profile.

StudentHub is an online platform that helps students find campus events, register for them, manage their profile and share feedback in one place.

Open the Register page and enter your name, email, enrollment number (for example 25aiml019), department and a password. Your enrollment number becomes your username.

Use the Login page with your email address or enrollment number and your password. Administrators use the same page and are taken to the admin panel.

At least 6 characters with one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter and one number. Longer passwords are stronger.

Open the Events page, find the event and press Register. You must be logged in. You can cancel the registration from the same page or from your dashboard.

Yes. Open your Profile to update your mobile number, department, hobbies and address, or to change your password.

Contact an administrator using the Contact page. They can reset your account. Once you are logged in you can change it any time from your Profile.

Feedback is stored securely in the database and is visible only to administrators, who use it to improve the portal.
